Privacy Policy

Jewelry Oblako · effective 9 October 2026

Who uses the app

Jewelry Oblako is a private tool used only by its owner to keep a working folder in sync between the owner's two computers. It is not offered to other people.

What Google data it accesses

  • The app requests only the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it the app can see and change only the files and folders it created itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files in the Drive.
  • It does not request access to email, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

How the data is used

  • Files are copied only between the owner's computers and the owner's own Google Drive, to keep the working folder the same on both computers.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. There are no servers, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's computers.

Sharing, selling, transfer

User data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Jewelry Oblako's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's computers until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Кратко по-русски

Программа видит только файлы, которые сама создала в Google Диске владельца (область drive.file), и копирует их между двумя компьютерами владельца. Данные никому не передаются и не продаются, серверов и аналитики нет. Отозвать доступ: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
